THE BTLED EMBLEM

The BTLED Organization prides itself on an emblem that reflects our core foundations: Heart, Gear, Hand, and Book. What sets the BTLED identity apart is its commitment to providing an inclusive, technical, and resource-driven education for all aspiring Industrial Arts educators.

True to its name, the Bravehearts Technology and Livelihood Education logo serves as a badge of honor and technical excellence. We are at the forefront of raising up leaders equipped with technical integration, practical craftsmanship, and a steadfast dedication to service. Together, we prepare the next generation of educators to create positive and lasting change for the betterment of the community.

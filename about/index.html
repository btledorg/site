Vision & Mission

Vision

To be a leading student organization that nurtures innovative, skilled, and service-driven future educators who embody excellence and integrity in the field of education.

Mission

The BTLED Organization is committed to developing the professional, academic, and personal growth of its members by:

  • Fostering innovation, creativity, and leadership;
  • Promoting collaboration and community engagement; and
  • Strengthening technical knowledge and pedagogical skills aligned with the standards of the College of Education and the University of Rizal System.

Objectives

  1. To provide activities that enhance the knowledge, skills, and competencies of BTLED students;
  2. To uphold the values of service, leadership, and professionalism among members;
  3. To support the College of Education's programs and initiatives;
  4. To foster unity, cooperation, and camaraderie among students; and
  5. To promote the organization as a platform for advocacy, creativity, and innovation.

Republic Act No. 10173 Declaration

1. Statement of Policy

The Bravehearts Technology And Livelihood Education Organization at the University of Rizal System (URS) - Cainta Campus is fully committed to protecting the privacy of its members, officers, and portal users. We recognize our legal responsibilities under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR), and the issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC).

2. General Privacy Principles

In processing your personal information, the BTLED Organization strictly adheres to the following core principles established by RA 10173:

  • Transparency: Students are informed of the nature, purpose, and extent of the processing of their personal data through consent forms and our platform's Privacy Policy.
  • Legitimate Purpose: We only process data for valid, declared, and specific organizational purposes, such as event management, member verification, and academic support.
  • Proportionality: The processing of information is adequate, relevant, suitable, and not excessive in relation to the declared organizational purpose.

3. Rights of the Data Subject

As a student and a "Data Subject" under Philippine law, you are entitled to the following rights regarding the personal information you submit to the BTLED portal:

  • Right to be Informed: To know whether your personal data is being, or has been, processed, and the purpose of such processing.
  • Right to Object: To withhold consent or object to the processing of your data, particularly for purposes not directly related to official academic or organizational functions.
  • Right to Access: To demand reasonable access to your profile data and submission records.
  • Right to Rectification: To dispute inaccuracies or errors in your data and have the organization correct it immediately (facilitated via the "Edit Profile" feature).
  • Right to Erasure or Blocking: To suspend, withdraw, or order the blocking, removal, or destruction of your personal data from our active systems upon graduating, transferring, or officially leaving the organization.
  • Right to Data Portability: To obtain a copy of your data in an electronic or structured format if technically feasible.

4. Organizational and Technical Security Measures

To safeguard your personal information against accidental loss, unauthorized access, and unlawful processing, the BTLED Organization implements the following measures:

  • Data Minimization: Our web forms and local storage scripts are designed to collect only the absolute minimum data required for a specific task.
  • Restricted Access: Access to master lists, file drives, and submission sheets is strictly limited to authorized Executive Officers and Faculty Advisers on a need-to-know basis.
  • Platform Security: The portal utilizes secure HTTPS protocols. Data stored locally in your browser (`localStorage`) is isolated and not transmitted to unauthorized third-party analytics trackers.

5. Data Retention and Disposal

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the declared purposes or as required by university guidelines. Once a student officially graduates, transfers, or the data is no longer needed for organizational tracking, all associated digital records, uploaded files, and staging folders are securely deleted from our Google Workspace drives to prevent indefinite data hoarding.

6. Data Protection Officer (DPO) Contact

The Executive Board designates an officer (typically the incumbent President or Secretary, overseen by the Faculty Adviser) to act as the primary point of contact for compliance matters. If you wish to exercise any of your rights under RA 10173, or if you have concerns regarding how your data is handled, please contact us immediately:

  • Help Center: BTLED Official Support
